Situational Analysis

Adequate nutrition is widely recognized as an essential element for a healthy and productive lifestyle. However, poor nutrition, which leads to preventable health challenges, remains a critical global challenge. Over two billion persons worldwide suffer from micronutrient deficiencies, with many at risk of problems resulting from defects in vitamins A and B, iodine, zinc and iron.

The challenges continue to rise despite the recent rigorous food and nutrition intervention programs to tackle protein and energy malnutrition (PEM) and micronutrient deficiency. Compromised diets that mainly rely on starchy foods such as maize products and bread; without other rich sources of essential protein, vitamins and minerals are the leading factors contributing to the burden of morbidity and mortality associated with malnutrition. Among the township, city, rural and peri-urban resident populations of sub-Saharan Africa, South African inclusive.
